## Robotics research

My research at Örebro University’s Machine Perception and Interaction Lab has focused on how robots interpret scenes, reason about feasible actions, and plan tasks.

- **Robot software integration.** Co-first author of [ViPlan]({{ '/projects/viplan/' | relative_url }}), a pipeline connecting visual grounding, vision-language reasoning, symbolic planning, and ROS 2 execution. The project was evaluated in simulation and on a Franka Panda.
- **Sim-to-real manipulation.** Co-author of [MEMENTO]({{ '/projects/memento/' | relative_url }}), which evolved executable policies in simulation and demonstrated transfer to a physical Franka Panda.
- **Ongoing research.** [Mode-aware 3D affordance learning]({{ '/projects/mode-aware-affordance/' | relative_url }}) uses paired object point clouds. Real-robot deployment is in progress.
- **Robot learning.** First author of [object affordance distillation]({{ '/projects/affordance-distillation/' | relative_url }}), which uses model-generated labels and explanations to train a predictor of feasible object interactions.
- **Embodied AI.** First author of [affordance-based goal imagination]({{ '/projects/goal-imagination/' | relative_url }}), which studies how object knowledge can help generate feasible visual goals.
