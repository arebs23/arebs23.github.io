Robotics software · Robot learning · Embodied AI

Victor Aregbede

I work on how robots turn observations and instructions into plans they can execute.

My work spans vision-language task planning, sim-to-real manipulation, and 3D object affordance learning. With my collaborators, I evaluated ViPlan on a Franka Panda and transferred an evolved Tower of Hanoi policy from simulation to a physical arm in MEMENTO.
