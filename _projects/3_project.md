**Role:** First author on the ICDL 2024 paper.

## Approach

An embodied agent needs to judge whether an action is feasible in the current scene before imagining its outcome. The team studied where image generators made errors in that judgment and explored using a large language model's knowledge about objects and their uses to inform goal image generation.

## Evaluation

The work examined whether models could distinguish plausible from implausible actions and depict the outcomes of feasible actions in context.
